How to edit any post — the 30-second version
1
Drop your photo
Drag any event photo straight onto the gray image area in a template. It snaps in and sticks — no upload step.
2
Click any text to edit
Click the title, date, venue, category chip or price pill and type over it. Keep the category in CAPS and the title short — that's what makes the brand look consistent.
3
Export & post
Save the card as an image, paste the matching caption above, drop your link in bio, and schedule it for the time on the calendar.
A few rules to keep it on-brand:  Coral (#EF4E44) is for accents & CTAs only — never large text blocks.  ·  One category chip per post, always in caps.  ·  Always end with the @trenevents handle + a link-in-bio nudge.  ·  Never put the event time/place in the caption ONLY — it has to live on the image too, since most people don't expand the caption.
