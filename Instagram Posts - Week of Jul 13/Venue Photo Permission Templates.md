# Venue Photo Permission Templates

Use these when you want to keep using a venue's photo instead of swapping to your own or a gradient card. Fill in the [bracketed] parts and send. Most venues say yes — it's free publicity for them.

---

## Email version

**Subject:** Featuring [Event Name] on TrenEvents — quick photo question

Hi [Venue/Organizer Name],

I run TrenEvents (trenevents.com), a local events guide covering Lancaster, York, and Harrisburg — we're featuring **[Event Name]** on [Date] and would love to use the photo from [where you found it, e.g. "your website" or "your event page"] alongside the listing.

Would that be alright — and OK if we do the same for your future events too, so we're not asking every time? Happy to credit and link back to you however you'd like, or swap in a different photo if you'd prefer we use something else.

Thanks for putting on a great event — hope to send some traffic your way!

[Your name]
TrenEvents
trenevents.com

## Instagram DM version (shorter)

Hi! I run TrenEvents (@trenevents), a local PA events guide. We're featuring **[Event Name]** and would love to use the photo from your page/site — ok to use it (and photos from your future events too) with a credit + link back to you? Happy to swap it for something else if you'd rather. Thanks either way, looks like a great event! 🎉

---

## Why the "future events too" line matters

Asking this way means a single "sure, go ahead" covers everything they host going forward — not just the one event you originally asked about. If you only ask about one event, treat their yes as good for that one only. If a venue's reply doesn't clearly say yes to future events (e.g. they only answered about the one you asked), don't assume it extends — ask explicitly next time or note it as one-time only.

## If they say yes

Tell Claude which venue said yes, whether it covers future events or just the one you asked about, and the date — it'll get logged in `venue-contacts.csv` so you're never prompted to email that venue again (for ongoing approvals), and so you have a record if anyone ever asks. If they gave you a preferred credit line or handle, mention that too.

## If they say no, or don't respond in a few days

Just use a gradient card via the Post Studio tool, or ask Claude to generate one — no need to wait on a response before posting. Swap in the real photo later if permission comes through.

## If someone asks you to take a photo down after the fact

Do it right away, no pushback — email bjdoersom@gmail.com or just tell Claude which event, and it'll be swapped or removed within the day. This is also exactly the practice described on the trenevents.com/image-credits page, so keeping it fast and no-friction matters.
