Photo Credits & Image Policy

Where our event photos come from, and how to reach us about one.

Last updated: July 12, 2026

The short version: Every photo on TrenEvents is used to promote the specific public event it depicts — nothing more. If you're a venue, organizer, or photographer and want a photo credited, corrected, or taken down, email us and we'll handle it within 48 hours, no questions asked.

Where Our Photos Come From

TrenEvents features photos from a few sources:

  • Original photography taken by TrenEvents at events we cover
  • Photos provided directly by event hosts and venues for promotional use
  • Publicly available promotional images from an event's own listing, venue website, or press page

Every photo is used solely to illustrate the event listing it appears next to — never resold, never used in advertising unrelated to that event, and never used to represent TrenEvents as the photo's creator.

Credit & Attribution

Where we can, we link back to the event's official page or venue from the listing itself, so anyone who wants to see more can find the original source. If you'd like a specific photo credit line added — a name, a business, a link — just ask and we'll add it.

Request a Credit, Correction, or Takedown

If you own the rights to a photo on TrenEvents and would prefer it not be used, or want it credited differently, email us at bjdoersom@gmail.com with a link to the event listing. We'll add a credit, swap the photo, or remove it — typically within 48 hours.

For Venues & Event Organizers

Prefer we use a specific photo, or want to send one directly? We'd rather use exactly what you'd choose. Reach out at bjdoersom@gmail.com or through our Event Hosts page.
